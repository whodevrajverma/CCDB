# CCDB: Cervical Cancer gene DataBase

Welcome to the official repository and documentation overview for the **Cervical Cancer gene DataBase (CCDB)**, a manually curated catalog of experimentally validated genes and microRNAs involved in the various stages of cervical carcinogenesis[cite: 1]. This resource serves as a specialized platform for researchers and clinicians to explore the molecular mechanisms underlying one of the most common malignancies affecting women worldwide[cite: 1].

**Web Server:** [http://crdd.osdd.net/raghava/ccdb](http://crdd.osdd.net/raghava/ccdb)[cite: 1]

## About the Database

CCDB is the first specialized resource of its kind, developed to address the lack of integrated data focused specifically on cervical cancer[cite: 1]. It consolidates information on genes linked to cancer causation processes such as methylation, gene amplification, mutation, polymorphism, and changes in expression levels[cite: 1].

The database integrates data from:
*   **Primary Literature:** Extensive manual searches of the PubMed database[cite: 1].
*   **External Repositories:** Data integrated from Homologene, HPRD, HGNC, and PharmaGKB[cite: 1].

---

## Key Features

### Comprehensive Dataset
*   **537 unique genes** involved in different stages of cervical carcinogenesis[cite: 1].
*   Experimentally validated **microRNAs (miRNAs)** with documented target genes and altered expression profiles[cite: 1].
*   **69 hypermethylated genes**, providing more extensive coverage than general methylation databases like PubMeth[cite: 1].

### Rich Annotations
Each record includes:
*   **Gene Architecture:** Schematic views of exon-intron structures[cite: 1].
*   **Sequence Data:** mRNA, CDS, and protein sequences, including various isoforms[cite: 1].
*   **Functional Insights:** Gene Ontology (GO) terms (molecular function and biological process)[cite: 1].
*   **Evolutionary Context:** Homology relationships and multiple sequence alignments across eukaryotic genomes[cite: 1].
*   **Supporting Evidence:** Manually curated literature references with PubMed IDs (PMIDs)[cite: 1].

### Built-in Tools
*   **Customized BLAST:** Search user-defined queries against database sequences to characterize orphan or homologous sequences[cite: 1].
*   **Search & Browse:** Query by gene name, ID, or chromosome, or browse by alphabetical order and biological category[cite: 1].

---

## Overview

CCDB is organized into six primary relational tables to ensure data integrity and ease of access:
1.  **Gene Detail:** Mapping between different gene and protein identifiers[cite: 1].
2.  **Homology:** Orthology relationships derived from Homologene[cite: 1].
3.  **Reference:** Manually curated evidence and sample collection details[cite: 1].
4.  **GO Table:** Functional classifications[cite: 1].
5.  **Sequence:** Location and sequences for mRNA, CDS, and proteins[cite: 1].
6.  **Structure:** 3D protein structure information[cite: 1].

---

## Applications

*   **Molecular Discovery:** Identifying potential biomarkers for cervical cancer progression[cite: 1].
*   **Comparative Oncology:** Comparing genes common across different cancers (e.g., lung or prostate) versus those unique to the cervix[cite: 1].
*   **Therapeutic Research:** Understanding enriched biological processes like signal transduction (26%) and nucleic acid metabolism (17%) to develop novel strategies[cite: 1].
